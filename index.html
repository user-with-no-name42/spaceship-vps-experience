My experience with Spaceship

The provider has no way to undo the consequences of its own mistakes.

My Spaceship account was suddenly blocked without warning. I lost access to both my account and my server.

I completed verification, and my account access was restored. But my original VPS was not. Support said that neither the server nor its data could be recovered.

The agent initially confirmed that the restriction was a mistake. He later explained it as an automated security check. That explanation did not change the outcome.

Escalating the issue to a supervisor changed nothing. No replacement or compensation was offered. The explanation: “However, this is how our system works, and it cannot be changed.”

My account access was restored. My server was not. The data could not be recovered. No compensation was offered. That was the extent of the help available: I was left with the loss.

That is what I would have to accept before trusting Spaceship with anything important.
